Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025  ·  Spook Intellect LLC  ·  STEM Heroes

✅ COPPA Compliant

📋 Overview

STEM Heroes is an educational flashcard and quiz application featuring 188 African American pioneers in Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics. It is a product of Spook Intellect LLC.

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with the STEM Heroes application and website (stemheroesapp.com). We are committed to protecting the privacy of all users, especially children.

The short version: STEM Heroes does not collect, store, or share personal information from students. The app runs entirely in your browser with no logins, no accounts, and no tracking.

👶 Children's Privacy (COPPA)

STEM Heroes is designed for use by K–12 students, including children under the age of 13. We take children's privacy seriously and comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

  • We do not collect personal information from children under 13.
  • We do not require user accounts, logins, or registration to use the app.
  • We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, or behavioral advertising on the student-facing app.
  • We do not display third-party advertisements to students.
  • Students can use the full app completely anonymously.

School administrators and educators using the app in a classroom setting do not need to obtain parental consent for students to access STEM Heroes, as the app collects no student data whatsoever.

📊 What Data We Collect

From students using the app: None. The STEM Heroes flashcard and quiz application stores no user data. All interactions (card flips, quiz answers, category filters) happen locally in the user's browser and are not transmitted to any server.

From educators and administrators submitting the contact form: When a school or district representative fills out our contact/inquiry form, we collect:

  • Name and professional title
  • School or district name
  • Professional email address
  • License type of interest
  • Any information voluntarily shared in the message field

This information is used solely to respond to licensing inquiries and is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties.

From website visitors (stemheroesapp.com): Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may collect standard server logs (IP addresses, browser type, pages visited) as part of normal web hosting operations. We do not use this data for marketing or tracking purposes. See GitHub's Privacy Statement for details.

🍪 Cookies & Tracking

The STEM Heroes student app does not use cookies or any tracking technologies.

The stemheroesapp.com website (landing page) may use minimal, necessary cookies associated with our contact form provider (Formspree). These cookies do not track student users and are only present on the marketing/landing page, not in the educational app itself.

🔒 Data Security

Because STEM Heroes collects virtually no user data, the risk of a data breach involving student information is minimal by design. The information submitted through our contact form is transmitted securely via HTTPS and processed by Formspree, which maintains industry-standard security practices.

We retain educator inquiry data only as long as necessary to respond to and fulfill licensing requests.

🏫 School & District Licensing

Schools and districts that purchase a STEM Heroes license do not provide us with student data. Licensed schools receive access to the same browser-based application available to all users, along with curriculum alignment documents, lesson plan templates, and support resources.

No student roster, student names, student email addresses, or any other student personally identifiable information (PII) is ever transmitted to Spook Intellect LLC as part of a school or district license.

🔗 Third-Party Services

STEM Heroes uses the following third-party services in limited capacities:

  • GitHub Pages — website hosting. Privacy Policy
  • Formspree — contact form processing (educator inquiries only). Privacy Policy
  • Google Fonts — typography (loads font files from Google servers). Privacy Policy
  • Wikimedia Commons — historical photographs displayed on hero cards (public domain / Creative Commons licensed images).

We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any other behavioral tracking or advertising technology.

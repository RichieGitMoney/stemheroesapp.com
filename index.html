What is STEM Heroes?

Representation that
Inspires the Next Generation

Research shows that students perform better and persist longer in STEM when they see people who look like them in those fields. STEM Heroes puts 188 real African American scientists, engineers, mathematicians, and innovators directly into students' hands.

📚
Interactive Flashcards
Flip cards reveal each hero's key achievement and a surprising fun fact. Real photos where available, vivid illustrations throughout.
🎯
Built-in Quiz Mode
Three question types keep students engaged. Tracks streaks and scores, encouraging healthy competition and repeated learning.
🔍
Search & Filter
Browse by STEM category — Science, Technology, Engineering, Math, Medicine, and Space. Search by name or field instantly.
🌐
Works Everywhere
Runs in any browser on Chromebooks, iPads, phones, and desktops. No app download required for web version. Coming to iOS and Android.
🏛️
188 Real Heroes
From Benjamin Banneker (1731) to Kizzmekia Corbett's COVID-19 vaccine — spanning 300 years of Black excellence in STEM.
👶🏾
All Ages
Written to engage students from elementary through high school. Simple enough for young learners, substantive enough for older students.
For Educators

Built for the Classroom.
Aligned to Real Standards.

STEM Heroes isn't just inspiring — it's educationally defensible. Districts can justify the purchase because the content maps directly to standards they're already required to meet.

  • 🔬
    Next Generation Science Standards (NGSS) Heroes organized by science and engineering practices; supports NOS (Nature of Science) instruction across all grade bands.
  • 📖
    Common Core ELA — Informational Text Each card functions as a biographical informational text, supporting RI.3–RI.8 reading standards for nonfiction.
  • 🏛️
    Black History / African American Studies Standards Directly addresses state-mandated Black history curriculum requirements. Covers historical through contemporary figures.
  • 🤝
    Social-Emotional Learning (SEL) Builds identity, belonging, and growth mindset by showing students what people who look like them have achieved.
  • 💻
    Computer Science & Career Awareness Expands student understanding of STEM careers — especially for students who have never seen Black professionals in these fields.
